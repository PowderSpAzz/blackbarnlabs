Overview

Independent lab. Close to the metal.

BlackBarn Labs is an independent research and prototyping lab focused on the intersection of hardware, firmware, and software systems.

Over the past year we’ve designed, built, and iterated on a range of practical projects — from custom embedded controllers and sensor systems to full-stack tools and applied R&D prototypes. We work hands-on with real hardware (microcontrollers, custom PCBs, power systems, and mechanical assemblies) and pair it with the software needed to make those systems reliable and usable.

We take ideas from napkin sketch through working prototype, with a preference for clean, maintainable solutions that actually survive real-world use. Current work includes ongoing hardware/firmware platforms, internal tools, and applied systems that sit at the boundary between physical devices and digital control.

BlackBarn Labs is intentionally small and independent. That lets us move quickly, stay close to the metal, and focus on the work itself rather than process.
