Support

Arrr You Hungry

Arrr You Hungry finds restaurants near you and picks one at random, so you stop arguing about where to eat. Here is how to get help with it.

The short version

  • Email chris@blackbarnlabs.com and a person reads it.
  • Tell us your iPhone model and your iOS version if you can — it helps a lot.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no subscriptions. Nothing to cancel, and nothing to buy inside the app.

02 · What the app does

Open the app and it looks for restaurants around you, then picks one at random from what it finds. You set how far out it should look. If you would rather search somewhere else, type a city or port name instead of using your location.

Three buttons do the work:

  • Aye saves the pick.
  • Walk the plank hides that place for good, so it stops coming up.
  • Set Sail opens directions to it in Apple Maps.

03 · Location

The first time you search, iOS asks whether the app may use your location. It is used only while the app is open, and only to find restaurants nearby. The app does not run location in the background and does not build a history of where you have been.

If you tapped Don't Allow, or nothing comes up, turn it back on in iOS Settings › Arrr You Hungry › Location, or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

You can leave location off entirely and type a city or port name instead. Everything else in the app keeps working without it.

04 · The search radius

The radius is how far from your starting point the app is allowed to look. A small radius keeps you close by; a wide one gives it more places to choose from. It applies to the search, not to the whole app, so you can change it whenever you like.

If a search comes back empty, the usual cause is a radius that is too tight for a quiet area, or a typed place name that Apple Maps does not recognise. Widen the radius or add the state or country to the name.

05 · Saved and hidden places

When you tap Aye, the pick is logged as saved. When you tap Walk the plank, that place is hidden and will not come up in a search again.

Both lists live in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us and not synced to an account, because there is no account. If you delete the app, both lists go with it. If iCloud Backup is on for your device, iOS may include app data in your own device backup.

06 · Directions

Set Sail hands the chosen restaurant to Apple Maps and opens it. From there Apple Maps handles the route, and what it does with the request is covered by Apple's privacy policy rather than ours.

07 · Connection and privacy

The app's only network traffic is to Apple Maps, through MapKit, which is built into iOS. Your coordinate — or the city or port you typed — the radius you set, and a plain category word go to Apple so it can return places near you. That is the whole of it.

There are no servers of ours in the picture, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting. Nothing is sent to BlackBarn Labs, and we could not receive it if it were: the app has no server of its own. The privacy policy has the detail.

08 · Price and purchases

Arrr You Hungry is a one‑time purchase, and there are no subscriptions and no ads.

Every feature is included. Nothing to buy inside the app, and nothing to restore — if you reinstall or move to a new iPhone, it is all there.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, because Apple takes the payment. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

09 · Requirements

Arrr You Hungry needs an iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. There is no iPad version, no Mac version, and no Apple Watch or Apple TV version. It needs a connection to reach Apple Maps; without one, a search cannot run.

10 · If something goes wrong

  • No restaurants found. Widen the radius, check that location is on for the app, or type the city or port name instead.
  • A place keeps coming back that you dislike. Tap Walk the plank to hide it for good.
  • Set Sail does nothing. It opens Apple Maps, so check that Maps is installed and working.
  • A typed place is not recognised. Add more detail, such as the state or country.
  • Nothing works at all. Check your connection, then force the app closed and reopen it. If it still fails, email us.

11 · Privacy

We collect nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third‑party code. The only service the app talks to is Apple's Maps, and nothing comes to us. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
