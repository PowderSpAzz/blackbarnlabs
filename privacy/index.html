Privacy notice

blackbarnlabs.com

Effective November 13, 2026 · BlackBarn Labs, Indiana

This notice covers this website, blackbarnlabs.com, run by BlackBarn Labs in Indiana. Our apps have their own privacy policies: Rosette Lab and Arrr You Hungry. Nothing on this page changes those.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking pixels, and no cookies set by this site.
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which Cloudflare describes as cookieless.
  • Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • If you chat with Rosie, your messages go to xAI to write the replies.
  • The site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps short‑lived request logs.
  • If you use a Notify me form, we keep your email, the product you picked and the date, and nothing else. Email us to be removed.

01 · Visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

Cloudflare adds a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com to our pages. It reports page views and page‑load timing to Cloudflare, so we can see roughly how many people visit and how fast the pages load. It sends the page address, the referring page, your browser type and timing measurements.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors. We only see totals in Cloudflare's dashboard. We can't see who you are, and we don't combine these numbers with anything else.

02 · Fonts (Google Fonts)

Our pages use the Barlow Condensed and Share Tech Mono typefaces, loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When a page loads, your browser asks Google for them, so Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google handles that under its own privacy policy. We get nothing back from Google about you.

03 · Talking to Rosie

Rosie is the chat assistant on our homepage. She only runs if you open the chat and send a message.

  • What is sent. Your message (up to 800 characters) goes to our server at /api/rosie, along with up to eight earlier messages from the same chat so Rosie can follow the conversation. Our server forwards them to xAI (api.x.ai), which writes the reply. xAI handles that text under its own privacy policy.
  • What your browser keeps. When you open the chat, your browser stores a random chat ID (bbl-rosie-session) in its local storage and sends it with each message. It isn't linked to your name, and our server doesn't save it. You can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • What we keep. Our server doesn't save conversations. There's one exception: if you ask Rosie to pass a note to Chris, or she decides your message is meant for him, she writes a short note with your message and any name you give. That note is written to our Cloudflare logs, which are deleted after at most 7 days. The site's code can also keep notes for up to 90 days in Cloudflare storage. That storage isn't connected right now, and we'll update this page before we connect it.
  • Please don't type passwords, payment details or anything sensitive into the chat.

04 · Hosting and logs (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare hosts this site and delivers every page. Like any web host, it has to process your IP address and request details to serve the page and protect the site from abuse. Our site's server logs each request: the address requested, the response, the time and the Cloudflare location that handled it. These server logs are kept for at most 7 days. Cloudflare also asks browsers to report connection errors back to Cloudflare (Network Error Logging). Cloudflare handles all of this under its own privacy policy.

05 · Email

Emails to chris@blackbarnlabs.com are delivered to a mailbox hosted by Zoho Mail. We keep your email, address and anything you send so we can reply. If you want your messages deleted, ask us and we will delete them. We don't add you to any mailing list.

06 · Notify-me signups

The pages for products we're still building (the Sentry Turret, Garden Buddy and the CO2 Cat De-Turret) have a "Notify me" form. It's the only form on this site.

  • What we store. Your email address, which product you picked, and the date and time you signed up. Nothing else: not your name, IP address, browser or location. Signing up for the same product twice keeps one entry.
  • Why. Only to email you news about the product you picked, such as when it's ready. It isn't a newsletter, and we never sell, rent or share the list.
  • Where. In a Cloudflare D1 database in our own Cloudflare account. Cloudflare stores it for us as our host, under its privacy policy.
  • How long. Until you ask us to remove it, or 12 months after that product launches or is dropped, whichever comes first. Then we delete it.
  • How to unsubscribe. Email chris@blackbarnlabs.com from the address you signed up with and say "unsubscribe". We'll delete your entry, normally within 7 days, and reply to confirm. Any email we send about a product will say how to unsubscribe.
  • Spam protection. The form has a hidden field that only bots fill in, and it limits how many tries one connection can make in a few minutes. That check looks at your IP address in memory while your request is handled. It isn't saved.

07 · What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No cookies set by this site, and no tracking pixels.
  • No accounts or logins for visitors.

08 · Your choices

  • Browse without using Rosie. The rest of the site works without her.
  • Content blockers that block cloudflareinsights.com or Google Fonts won't break the site. The text falls back to other fonts.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

09 · Changes to this notice

If what this site collects changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change goes live. The current version is always the one at this address.
