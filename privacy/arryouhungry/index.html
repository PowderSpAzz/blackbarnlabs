Privacy policy

Arrr You Hungry

Effective October 28, 2026 · BlackBarn Labs, Indiana

This policy covers the Arrr You Hungry app for iPhone (bundle ID com.blackbarnlabs.arrryouhungry), made by BlackBarn Labs in Indiana. It explains what the app does with your information, in plain words. The whole answer fits in the box below.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third‑party code.
  • There is no account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
  • Your searches go to Apple Maps, and only to Apple. They never reach a server of ours, because we do not run one.
  • Your saved places and hidden places stay on your device.
  • Our App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

01 · What we collect

Nothing. BlackBarn Labs does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share personal data through Arrr You Hungry.

Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. There is no account, no user ID, no profile and no tracking across other apps or websites. Nothing about you or where you have looked is stored on a server belonging to us.

We run no server of our own. The app has nowhere to send your information even if it wanted to.

02 · How the app uses your device

Location

Arrr You Hungry asks for your location so it can find restaurants near you. It uses it only while the app is open, and only to run that search. The app does not track you in the background and does not keep a history of where you have been.

You do not have to give it your location. If you decline, or if you would rather not use it, you can type a city or port name instead and search from there.

You can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings › Arrr You Hungry › Location, or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. Typed searches keep working without it.

What is stored on your device

Two things are kept in the app's own storage on your device: the places you saved, and the list of places you hid so they stop coming up. Both are deleted when you delete the app. They are not sent to us and are not backed up to any server of ours. If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device, the system may include app data in your own device backup; that backup belongs to your Apple account, not to us, and we cannot see it.

03 · Third parties

There are no third‑party servers, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash‑reporting service, no attribution service and no social login. The app does not share, sell or disclose your information to any third party.

The one outside service it uses is Apple's own Maps, through MapKit, which is part of iOS. That is covered in the next section.

04 · What leaves your device, and where it goes

The app talks to Apple, and to nobody else. There are two ways that happens:

  • Finding restaurants. Your coordinate — or the city or port name you typed — the search radius you set, and a plain category word such as restaurant, food, cafe or bakery are sent to Apple Maps so it can return places near you.
  • Directions. If you tap Set Sail, the app hands the chosen place to Apple Maps and the Maps app opens. From that point the Maps app handles it, under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Every one of those requests goes to Apple. None of it goes to BlackBarn Labs, and we never receive it. We do not keep a copy, and we cannot look up where you searched or what you picked.

There is no other networking code in the app. Nothing phones home, checks for updates, reports a crash or sends a usage ping.

05 · How long we keep things

We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to retain. On your device, the saved list and the hidden list stay in the app's storage until you delete the app. Apple handles its own retention for MapKit requests as described in Apple's privacy policy.

06 · Your control, and how to revoke or delete

Because nothing is held on a server of ours, there is no deletion request to file and no account to close. Everything is in your hands:

  • Turn off location in iOS Settings › Arrr You Hungry › Location. Typed searches still work without it.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app. The saved list and the hidden list live in the app's storage, and iOS removes that storage with the app.

If you would like us to confirm in writing that we hold no data about you, email us and we will.

07 · Sharing

Arrr You Hungry does not post anywhere on your behalf, does not have a social feed, and does not contact anyone for you. Opening directions hands the chosen place to Apple Maps, which is the only hand‑off the app makes.

08 · Purchases

Arrr You Hungry is a one‑time purchase, with no ads and no subscriptions. Payment and refunds are handled by Apple under your Apple account; we never see your payment details. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

There is nothing to buy inside the app. Every feature is included in the price.

09 · Children

Because we collect nothing, we collect nothing from children either. There are no ads, no chat, no way to contact anyone through the app, and nothing to buy inside it. Parents should still use iOS Screen Time and purchase controls as they see fit.

10 · Requirements

Arrr You Hungry runs on iPhone with iOS 18.0 or later. No account is required. It needs a connection only to reach Apple Maps for a search or to open directions.

11 · Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches you. The current version is always the one at this address. We will not quietly broaden what we collect.
