Privacy policy

Rosette Lab

Effective October 28, 2026 · BlackBarn Labs, Indiana

This policy covers the Rosette Lab app for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.blackbarnlabs.rosettelab), made by BlackBarn Labs in Indiana. It explains what the app does with your information, in plain words. The whole answer fits in the box below.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third‑party code.
  • There is no account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
  • Your drawings, gallery and settings stay on your device.
  • The microphone runs only in the Listen tab and three Sound modes. Audio is analyzed live and is never saved or sent.
  • Rosette Lab works offline. No connection is needed to draw, decode a code, or solve a puzzle. Only buying or restoring Code Maker needs one.
  • Our App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

01 · What we collect

Nothing. BlackBarn Labs does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share personal data through Rosette Lab.

Specifically, the app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone audio, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. There is no account, no user ID, no profile and no tracking across other apps or websites.

We run no server of our own, and the app never sends your drawings, photos or audio to anyone. The one time the app talks to the internet is when you buy or restore Code Maker, and that is the App Store handling the transaction for Apple, not us. Nothing about what you draw goes with it.

02 · How the app uses your device

Microphone

Rosette Lab asks for the microphone so it can hear pitch, volume and rhythm. Four places in the app use it: the Listen tab, and in the Sound tab the Listen, NamePlate and Hum to Unlock modes. The other six Sound modes play sound rather than listening for it, and nothing outside those four places touches the mic.

When you are in one of them:

  • Audio is captured into memory, on your device, and analyzed there in real time. Nothing is buffered longer than it takes to work out what the sound is.
  • Audio is never written to a file, never saved to disk and never sent anywhere — not to us and not to anyone else. The samples are discarded as soon as they have been analyzed.
  • Each of those four screens has a Stop control, so you can always end a listen yourself.
  • While the microphone is live, both iOS and Rosette Lab show an indicator on screen so you can always tell when the app is listening.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings › Rosette Lab › Microphone, or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. The rest of the app keeps working; only those four places need it.

Photos

The app uses Photos in two ways, both started by you:

  • Adding an image you exported to your photo library. The app asks for add-only access, which means it can save but cannot browse your library.
  • Reading a single photo you pick, so it can be turned into spiral line art. That photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded.

You can change or remove this access in iOS Settings › Rosette Lab › Photos.

Camera and location

Rosette Lab does not use your camera and does not use your location. It never asks for either.

What is stored on your device

Your designs and gallery, your puzzle progress and stars, and your preferences (such as ink style and last‑used settings) are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device, the system may include app data in your own device backup; that backup belongs to your Apple account, not to us, and we cannot see it.

Working offline

Decoder codes, the Rosette Bureau puzzles, drawing, photo conversion and sound analysis all work with no internet connection. Nothing is downloaded when you use them and nothing is uploaded. The only feature that uses a connection is the App Store purchase for Code Maker.

03 · Third parties

There are none. Rosette Lab contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash‑reporting service, no attribution service, no social login and no other third‑party code. Our app does not share, sell or disclose your information to any third party, because it has none to share.

To be explicit about what Apple's privacy rules ask for: no parent, subsidiary or related company has access to your data either, because no data leaves your device. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change ships, and any party that received data would be required to provide the same or equal protection described here.

04 · How long we keep things

We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to retain. Information on your device lasts until you delete it or delete the app.

  • Audio is never retained, not even for a moment beyond the live analysis.
  • Photos you pick are used for the conversion and not kept by us.
  • Exported PNG, SVG and GIF files go wherever you send them — your photo library, Files, or another app — and are then yours to keep or delete.

05 · Your control, and how to revoke or delete

Because nothing is held on a server, there is no deletion request to file and no account to close. Everything is in your hands:

  • Turn off the microphone in iOS Settings › Rosette Lab › Microphone.
  • Turn off photo access in iOS Settings › Rosette Lab › Photos.
  • Delete a design in the Gallery inside the app.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app. iOS removes its storage with it.

If you would like us to confirm in writing that we hold no data about you, email us and we will.

06 · Sharing

Rosette Lab can share an image, GIF or decoder code you made, using the standard iOS share sheet. Sharing only happens when you start it. Where the file goes after that is decided by the app you send it to, and that app's privacy policy applies to it from then on.

07 · Purchases

Rosette Lab is a one-time purchase, with no ads and no subscriptions. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple under your Apple account; we never see your payment details. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Inside the app there is one optional extra: Code Maker, $1.99 once, for writing your own message codes. It is bought through the App Store, and a Restore Purchases button brings it back on any device signed in to the same Apple account. If you never buy it, nothing is charged and the rest of the app works exactly the same. Any message you create stays on your device like everything else.

08 · Children

Rosette Lab is suitable for all ages, and because we collect nothing, we collect nothing from children either. There are no ads, no chat and no way to contact anyone through the app, and the only purchase is the optional Code Maker unlock described above. Rosette Lab is not in the App Store's Kids category; parents should still use iOS Screen Time and purchase controls as they see fit.

09 · Requirements

Rosette Lab runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.0 or later. No account is required. Every feature works offline except buying or restoring Code Maker.

10 · Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches you. The current version is always the one at this address. We will not quietly broaden what we collect.
